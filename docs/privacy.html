Privacy Policy

DropBall for iPhone · Last updated 5 November 2026

DropBall does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The game has no servers of its own and makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

Everything the game saves is stored locally on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere by the app:

  • Your best 20 scores, with the date each was set.
  • Your settings, e.g., whether music, sound effects or rumble are on.

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no copy for us to hold, because none is ever sent to us.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, DropBall submits your score to an Apple-operated leaderboard when a run ends, and can read the leaderboard back to show you global rankings.

That submission sends your score and your Game Center player identity to Apple, not to us. It is handled entirely by Apple's Game Center service and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your Apple Account, your email address, or your real name.

If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is submitted and the game is fully playable offline. You can sign out at any time in the iOS Settings app under Game Center.

Links out of the app

The Credits screen contains links to the websites of the people whose music, artwork and font the game uses. Tapping one opens it in your browser and leaves the app. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

DropBall is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children. It knowingly collects no personal information from children under 13, because it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it handles, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed before that version is released.
