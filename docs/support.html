DropBall

Support and help

DropBall is a one-thumb arcade game about falling. If something isn't working, or you just have a question, the answers below cover most of it, and if they don't, get in touch.

How to play

  • Steer: drag your thumb left or right anywhere on the screen. How far you drag from where you first touched sets how fast the ball moves sideways.
  • Drop faster: press and hold without moving. The ball speeds up and leaves a trail behind it.
  • Score: one point for every platform you get through.
  • The bar above the ball creeps down the whole time. Clearing a platform pushes it back up. If it reaches you, the run is over, so you can't stall.

Common questions

How do I turn off the music, sound or vibration?

Tap the gear icon on the title screen. Music, Sound Effects and Rumble each have their own switch, and changes take effect straight away.

Why don't I see the global leaderboard?

The GLOBAL SCORES option only appears once you're signed in to Game Center. If it's missing, open the Settings app, go to Game Center, and sign in. The rest of the game works exactly the same either way.

My global rank looks out of date.

The leaderboard is fetched once when you open it and kept for as long as you stay on that screen. Leave the screen and come back to refresh it. Scores you set while offline are submitted automatically the next time you're signed in and connected.

Where are my scores stored?

Your best 20 runs are kept on your device only. They aren't backed up to a server by the game, so deleting the app deletes them. If you've submitted to Game Center, those scores stay on the leaderboard under your Game Center account.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. The only thing that needs a connection is the global leaderboard.

Does the game use my data for anything?

No. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no accounts. See the privacy policy for the full detail.
